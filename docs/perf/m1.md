# S7 game-scene frame budget at view distance 8 (M1 content load)

Local frame-budget record for the S7 vertical-slice game scene. The acceptance
budget is **≤ 11.1 ms per frame at 90 Hz** (ADR-0010). This is the M1 content
load (streaming + chunk views + gameplay bridge), not the S6 calibration scene;
the calibration numbers stay in [`s6.md`](s6.md).

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Scene:** `unity/Cubeglass/Assets/Scenes/Game.unity` (committed; never
  regenerated by the per-commit lanes)
- **Machine:** AMD Ryzen AI 9 365 w/ Radeon 880M (20 logical CPUs, 1996 MHz),
  23 GiB RAM, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 Laptop GPU (7.9 GB), Windows 11
  10.0.26200
- **Toolchain:** Unity 6000.6.3f1, Direct3D 12 (level 12.2), PlayMode CLI lane
  (`unity test unity/Cubeglass --mode PlayMode --non-interactive`)
- **Test:** `GameFrameBudgetPlayModeTests.GameSceneHoldsTheFrameBudgetAtViewDistanceEight`
  (PlayMode suite, 40 tests total)

## Method

The measurement loads the committed game scene, redirects its store to a temp
world through the `GameBoot` seams, and streams the scene's desired set to
drained: with the slice's view distance 8, vertical radius 2 and one-chunk-
per-frame generation, **1,445 chunk views** are resident (the full 17×17×5
desired box) before the measured window starts. The overlay is off for the
first window; the second window enables it.

Each measured frame drives one deterministic slice tick
(`StreamingRuntime.Tick` → `GameplayBridge.Tick(1/60)`; auto-update is off) and
then renders both eye cameras into one 3840×1080 side-by-side `RenderTexture`
(**1920×1080 per eye**). The stopwatch interval contains the tick (streaming
scheduler, deferred generation, mesh upload, input sample, controller and
interaction) plus the two `Camera.Render()` calls; the render-only split is
recorded too. Each window warms up 32 frames and measures 600 frames;
p50/p95/p99 are nearest-rank over the 600 samples. The full report (including
the machine fields reported by `SystemInfo`) is written to
`%TEMP%\s7-game-frame-budget.json` by the test.

## Result

| Statistic | Overlay off frame (ms) | Overlay off render (ms) | Overlay on frame (ms) | Overlay on render (ms) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| mean | 1.392 | 1.214 | 1.411 | 1.230 |
| p50 | 1.293 | 1.114 | 1.309 | 1.132 |
| p95 | 2.141 | 1.898 | 2.177 | 1.930 |
| p99 | 2.749 | 2.547 | 2.875 | 2.645 |
| min | 1.012 | — | 0.994 | — |
| max | 3.867 | — | 6.765 | — |

"frame" is the full measured interval (slice tick + stereo render submission);
"render" is the two `Camera.Render()` calls alone. The difference (≈ 0.18 ms at
the mean) is the streaming + bridge CPU cost in the steady state. Enabling the
overlay moves the frame mean by 1.4 % and p99 by 4.6 %, all inside the
run-to-run spread of the headless lane.

Against the 11.1 ms budget the overlay-off window has **8.0×** headroom at the
mean and **4.0×** at p99; the overlay-on window has **7.9×** at the mean and
**3.9×** at p99. No measured statistic approaches the target.

## Caveats

- **CPU-side submission, not a presented frame.** The CLI PlayMode lane has no
  Game View and no swap-chain present; GPU completion and display cadence are
  outside the number (the same headless-lane caveat as S6). A machine GPU
  (RTX 5070 Laptop, D3D12) was present and the cameras rendered into the
  render target, but the measurement is CPU-side render submission.
- **RTX 5070 p99 on the glasses is HIL/player-build-verified.** The requirement
  that the M1 slice hold 90 Hz on the RTX 5070 when presented on the Luma Ultra
  cannot be proven from the headless lane; it is the S7 HIL checklist item
  ([`../questions/S7-HIL.md`](../questions/S7-HIL.md)), run against the built
  Windows player.
- **"Overlay on" is an enabled read path, not painted IMGUI.** `OnGUI` never
  executes in the lane, so the overlay's `GUI.Box`/`GUI.Label` paint cost is not
  in the number; the second window pins that enabling the overlay does not
  disturb the measured path.
- **Content shape.** 1,445 views are resident, but most chunks are air or
  unbroken underground rock with empty meshes; the visible surface is the
  17×17 column set. The number is the honest slice load, not a worst-case
  terrain.
- **Editor-process variance.** The lane runs in the editor with editor-loop
  scheduling, so the absolute numbers vary between runs; the gate is this
  recorded comparison against the budget, not an in-test timing assertion
  (the test fails only if the scene cannot be loaded or streamed to drained).
